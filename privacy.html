Privacy

This page covers this website only. The privacy notice for the app and for accounts is published here before sign-up opens.

What this website keeps

  • Nothing about you. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, and nothing loaded from other sites.
  • Forms send nothing. The contact, request and waitlist forms are not connected yet. What you type stays on your screen.
  • One setting in your browser. Whether Demo is on is stored on your own device, so it stays on as you move between pages. Turn it off, or clear this site's data, to remove it.
  • Your location, only if you ask. "Show beaches near me" asks your browser for your location to sort the list once. It stays in your browser: we never receive or store it.

Our host

Like any website, the company that hosts these pages keeps short technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to deliver and protect the site. Its details are in the Legal notice.

Questions

Email leo@ob.surf. We use what you send only to answer you. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In France that's the CNIL (cnil.fr).
