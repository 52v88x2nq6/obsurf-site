How it works

From the surf cam to your wrist.

01 · Crowds

Surfers become dots.

When the camera can see the lineup, everyone in the water becomes a dot on the map, grouped by peak. Each peak gets a word, a range and a face.

We count people, not who they are.

02 · Rides

Rides, tracked.

Rides are drawn on a detailed 3D map of the beach. The longer the ride, the further its colour runs.

05 · In the water

Your Watch points the way.

Start a session on Apple Watch and leave it on auto. Heading out, an arrow points you to a better peak on the same beach, with how far it is and how busy. At the peak, you see its crowd now, your time in the water and your distance.

Custom algorithms, built on data from multiple sources. Sources and credits
