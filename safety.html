Safety

In an emergency, call 112.

  • ob.surf is not a safety service. Always follow the lifeguards' flags and advice.
  • Our figures can be late, rough or wrong. A beach that looks quiet on ob.surf can be crowded, and conditions change fast.
  • Don't paddle out on our word alone. Look at the sea, ask the lifeguards, and know your limits.
  • Tide figures are a rough blend of our sources. Not for navigation.
