What earns each face

A face tells you how often a figure like this one has turned out right at this beach, and a beach shows only the faces its own record backs up. Sure is rare and slow to earn; expect Rough idea and Fairly sure most of the time.

When a figure fades

A figure fades as its newest information gets older, then goes, so an old figure never reads as a fresh one. We never show the time a figure is from, since each one blends several sources.

  • A live count fades after a few minutes (4 to 10), and goes at 10, until the camera counts again.
  • A crowd from reports fades once the newest report is over an hour old, and goes at 90 minutes.
  • The usual crowd doesn't fade: it comes from past days, not from now.
